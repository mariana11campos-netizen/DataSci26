🥁 Learn Drums

Build a beat while learning what each drum does.

Lesson 1: The basic beat Start with the kick on beats 1 and 3, and the snare on beats 2 and 4. Add hi-hats on every step to keep time.

What you're learning

Kick: The low “boom.” It gives the beat its foundation.
Snare: The sharp “crack.” It often lands on beats 2 and 4.
Hi-hat: The “tss” sound. It helps you keep a steady rhythm.
Tip: A 16-step row represents one bar. Try changing one sound at a time and listen to what changes.
